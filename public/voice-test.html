CareerOS Voice Interview - Test Harness

Dev-only test page. Not part of the product UI - just here to make manually testing the voice flow easy without scripting audio uploads through Postman.

1. Log in

2. Start interview

3. Interview

Once you finish speaking your answer, simply pause for 2 to 3 seconds and it will be submitted automatically - no need to click anything. You can still click "Stop recording" manually if you'd rather submit sooner. In Chrome/Edge, your words are transcribed live as you talk (shown below as you speak) - no upload wait when you stop. Other browsers fall back to uploading the recording for server-side transcription after you stop, which is slower.

Tip: watch the status line under the record button while speaking - if you see your words appearing live, the fast path is active.
